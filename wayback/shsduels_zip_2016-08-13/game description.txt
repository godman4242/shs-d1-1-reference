Slash your way into the world of Straw Hat Samurai: Duels, the side-scrolling samurai MMORPG!

Use your mouse/finger to draw a line and your samurai will perform his/her slashes based on the lines that you draw!

• Experience an epic story full of mystery, intrigue and action!
• Live in an ever-changing world with dynamic quests that evolves based on the actions of the players!
• Fight real-time PvP sword duels and participate in tournaments for honor, glory and riches!
• Command troops and fight strategic battles against rival Clans in the War Room!
